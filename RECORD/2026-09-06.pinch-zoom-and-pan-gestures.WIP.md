## 1. Goal & Context

Enable fluid touch gestures and trackpad navigation on the pixel art canvas in `CanvasStage`:
1. **Pinch to Zoom**: Pinching with two fingers on touchscreens or trackpads smoothly zooms in and out around the gesture center.
2. **Two-Finger Pan**: Dragging with two fingers on touchscreens pans the canvas viewport freely.
3. **Trackpad & Mouse Navigation**:
   - Trackpad pinch-to-zoom (detected via `wheel` event with `ctrlKey`).
   - Two-finger trackpad scroll to pan (`deltaX`, `deltaY`).
   - Middle-click / Space-drag pan support.
   - Quick reset button to recenter and reset zoom.
4. **Strict Component Isolation**: `CanvasStage.ts` only imports `helpers/` or other `components/`.

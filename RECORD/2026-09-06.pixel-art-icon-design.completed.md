## 1. Goal & Context

Design and integrate an authentic, iconic pixel art brand icon for **SpriteMotion**:
- Concept: A dynamic 16-bit pixel art hero mid-motion leaping between keyframe diamonds.
- Visual elements:
  - Articulated skeletal joints with glowing pivot points (representing hierarchical 2D transforms).
  - Cyan and magenta onion skinning ghost motion trails (representing keyframe animation and timing).
  - Golden central keyframe diamond along a curved trajectory.
  - Pixel typography and retro palette.
- Formats:
  - High-resolution asset (`public/assets/spritemotion_icon.jpg`).
  - Pixel-perfect crisp SVG vector icons (`src/icon-512.svg`, `src/icon-192.svg`) with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`.
  - App header logo and Welcome Screen hero badge integration.
- Strict Component Isolation: Verified zero non-helper imports across all modules in `src/components/`.

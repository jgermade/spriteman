## 1. Goal & Context

Enable multi-touch gestures and trackpad navigation on the pixel art viewport:
1. **Pinch-to-Zoom**: Two-finger pinch gesture smoothly zooms in and out (1x to 32x) on touch screens and trackpads (via `wheel` + `ctrlKey`).
2. **Two-Finger Pan**: Dragging with two fingers moves/pans the canvas viewport freely across the screen.
3. **Desktop Trackpad & Mouse Support**:
   - Two-finger trackpad scrolling seamlessly pans the canvas.
   - Middle-click and Shift+Left-drag pan options for desktop mouse users.
   - Floating HUD overlay showing real-time zoom level (`hud-zoom-badge`).
   - Quick "Reset" button and double-tap / double-click shortcut to center and reset view.
4. **Strict Component Isolation**: `CanvasStage.ts` imports exclusively from `../helpers/canvas.helper`.

---

## 2. Changes Implemented

### Helpers
- **`src/helpers/canvas.helper.ts`**:
  - `getTouchDistance`: Computes Euclidean distance between two touches for pinch calculation.
  - `getTouchMidpoint`: Computes midpoint of two touches for two-finger pan deltas.

### Components (Strictly Isolated)
- **`src/components/CanvasStage.ts`**:
  - Maintained `panX` and `panY` coordinate offsets and continuous `zoom` state.
  - Implemented `touchstart`, `touchmove`, `touchend`, `touchcancel` with `e.preventDefault()` to capture two-finger gestures without triggering page zoom or scroll.
  - Added trackpad `wheel` listener handling both pinch (`ctrlKey` / `metaKey`) and two-finger pan (`deltaX`, `deltaY`).
  - Added desktop mouse pan (middle mouse button or Shift+drag).
  - Added floating HUD overlay displaying zoom badge and `🎯 Reset` button.
  - Re-centered canvas rendering applying `ctx.translate(centerX + panX, centerY + panY)` and `ctx.scale(zoom, zoom)`.

### Styles
- **`src/index.html`**:
  - `.canvas-stage-root`: Full viewport fill, `touch-action: none`, grab/grabbing cursors.
  - `.canvas-hud-overlay`: Floating badges for current zoom and reset button.

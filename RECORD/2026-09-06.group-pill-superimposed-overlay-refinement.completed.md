# Group Pill Superimposed Overlay Refinement — Completed

## Goal / Context
Refine `.active-group-pill.in-reel` to be superimposed directly on top of intermediate frame buttons in the reel:
- Prevent intermediate buttons from pushing timeline reel height or causing multi-row vertical shifts.
- Keep the timeline reel perfectly aligned at 54px uniform height across all frames and groups.
- Position the pill with glassmorphic backdrop filter and centered alignment over intermediate cells.
- Allow clicking the body of the pill to instantly select and focus the start keyframe of the group.

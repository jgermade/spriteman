# Record: Canvas Light Theme Implementation

**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Status:** Completed  

---

## 1. Goal / Context

The user requested: *"usa un tema claro en el canvas"* (use a light theme in the canvas).
The objective was to transform the canvas stage, workbench viewport, artboard surface, pixel grid, and cursor highlights into a sleek, professional light theme while preserving crisp pixel art readability and contrast.

---

## 2. Tasks & Changes

### A. Viewport & HUD Styling (`src/index.html`)
- Updated `.canvas-viewport-panel` workbench backdrop to a soft studio light gradient (`radial-gradient(circle at center, #eef2f6 0%, #d8e2ed 100%)`).
- Restyled `.hud-zoom-badge`, `.btn-hud-tool`, and `.hud-hint` with frosted glass light pills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`), subtle slate borders (`#cbd5e1`), soft drop shadows, and high-contrast typography.

### B. Artboard & Grid Lines (`src/components/CanvasStage.ts`)
- Replaced dark artboard background with pure crisp white `#ffffff`.
- Added soft ambient drop shadow around the artboard (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)` blur 16) separating the canvas surface from the workbench.
- Updated pixel grid lines to soft slate (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` base grid, and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` zoom overlay grid).
- Updated artboard border to clean sky blue `#0284c7`.
- Adjusted onion skin ghost tinting to `#0284c7` with 0.32 alpha for clear visibility against white.

### C. Contrast & Cursor Highlights (`src/helpers/canvas.helper.ts`)
- Implemented dual-outline pixel hover cursor (`drawPixelCursor`) with an outer dark stroke (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.7)`) and inner white stroke (`#ffffff`), ensuring 100% visibility over both light artboard space and dark painted pixels.
- Updated pivot gizmo crosshairs to contrast cleanly on light surfaces.

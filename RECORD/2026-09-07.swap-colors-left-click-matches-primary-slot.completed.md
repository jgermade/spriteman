# RECORD: Left Click Strictly Matches Color in Slot 'I' When Swapping Colors

**Date**: 2026-09-07  
**Status**: Completed  
**Type**: Bug Fix & Palette Interaction Refinement  

---

## 1. Goal / Context
The user specified:
> *"al activar btn-swap-colors, el click izquierdo del ratón debería ser el color que tenga 'I' en ese momento"*  
> (When activating btn-swap-colors, left click of the mouse should be the color that has 'I' at that moment).

Previously, when the user pressed `Cmd`/`Alt` or toggled `btn-swap-colors`:
1. `swapPaletteColors()` already swapped `primaryColor` and `secondaryColor` in `canvasService`, making the new color active in slot 'I'.
2. However, `CanvasStage.html`'s `mousedown` handler contained legacy logic: `const isCmdAlt = e.metaKey || isCmdHeld ... const useSecondary = isRightClick || isCmdAlt; currentPaintColor = useSecondary ? colors.secondary : colors.primary;`.
3. This resulted in an unintentional double-swap: even though slot 'I' held the desired color, left-clicking while `Cmd`/`Alt` or swap was active evaluated `useSecondary = true` and applied `colors.secondary` instead of `colors.primary`.
4. Similarly, `canvasService.redraw()` recalculated hover cursor color using `(modifiers.isCmdHeld || modifiers.isAltHeld) ? this.secondaryColor : this.primaryColor`.

---

## 2. Tasks & Changes

### A. Canvas Mouse Interaction (`src/components/CanvasStage.html`)
- Simplified the painting color resolution in `mousedown`:
  ```typescript
  const colors = canvasService.getColors();
  const isRightClick = e.button === 2;
  currentPaintColor = isRightClick ? colors.secondary : colors.primary;
  ```
- Guaranteed that:
  - Left click (`button === 0`) unconditionally paints `colors.primary` (the color currently assigned to slot 'I').
  - Right click (`button === 2`) unconditionally paints `colors.secondary` (the color currently assigned to slot 'D').

### B. Hover Cursor Synchronization (`src/services/canvas.service.ts`)
- Simplified hover highlight resolution in `canvasService.redraw()`:
  ```typescript
  let hoverColor = this.primaryColor;
  if (hoverColor === '__eraser__') hoverColor = 'rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.5)';
  ```
- Ensures the hover cursor cell indicator always matches slot 'I' regardless of whether colors were swapped permanently (button click / `[X]`) or temporarily (`Cmd`/`Alt`).

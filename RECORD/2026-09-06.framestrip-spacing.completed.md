# Record: FrameStrip Toolbar and Reel Spacing Increase

**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Status:** Completed  

---

## 1. Goal / Context

The user requested: *"aumenta el espacio entre framestrip-toolbar y framestrip-reel"* (increase the space between framestrip-toolbar and framestrip-reel).
The objective was to improve the vertical separation and breathing room in the bottom timeline panel so the playback/action buttons and the frame thumbnail reel have a comfortable, well-balanced margin.

---

## 2. Tasks & Changes

- In `src/index.html`:
  - Increased `--timeline-height` from `125px` to `142px` to accommodate additional vertical padding without scrolling.
  - Updated `.framestrip-container`:
    - Changed `justify-content` from `space-between` to `flex-start`.
    - Added `gap: 0.85rem` between the toolbar and the reel.
    - Updated padding to `0.65rem 0.85rem`.
  - Added `flex-shrink: 0` to both `.framestrip-toolbar` and `.frames-reel` to prevent layout collapsing.
